01 — 关于游戏

Big Walk 是什么？

Big Walk 是一款合作在线多人解谜冒险游戏，主题是和朋友一起边走边聊。它由 House House——墨尔本的《捣蛋鹅》工作室——开发，Panic 发行，于 2026 年 8 月 4 日上线。

在 2 到 12 人的小队里，你们一起穿越一座灵感来自澳大利亚内陆的广袤手工岛屿，共同解谜、揭开秘密。游戏里没有任务标记、体力条或血条——每一次发现都由你们自己完成。

这个世界是手工打造的，每个角落都像有设计意图。岛上散布着无线电电台，每个电台播放专属歌曲，你可以把它带上旅途。没有大段的剧情说明——玩家们常会给角色起自己的昵称，甚至为谜题发明专属词汇。

游戏有明确的结局，并非无限游玩的类型；但和不同的朋友一起，它依然充满吸引力。

03 — 新手引导

怎么玩？

  1. 2 到 12 人，纯多人

    没有单机模式。Big Walk 完全围绕合作与交流设计。

  2. 房主选择世界版本

    房主会选 2 人、3 人或 4+ 人版本的世界，这也代表队伍的最低人数；房主维护世界存档，队友可随时加入或离开。部分谜题会随人数调整。

  3. 务必用近距离语音聊天

    游戏的核心就是和朋友交谈。改用 Discord 或 Steam 语音会绕开解谜的设计，让游戏变差。

  4. 手持道具

    对讲机、手电筒、扩音器——有用，但通常不是必需。背包空间有限：想把纸质地图拿出来看，得让队友帮你卸下背包。

  5. 没有任务标记

    没有任务标记、体力条或血条——靠探索、尝试，和队友一起想办法。

  6. 理想人数

    人少时游戏更专注协作；接近 12 人则是有趣的混乱。任何人数都好——开发者并没有设定所谓「最佳人数」。

01 — About

What is Big Walk?

Big Walk is a cooperative online multiplayer puzzle adventure about walking and talking with friends. It was developed by House House — the Melbourne studio behind Untitled Goose Game — and published by Panic. The game launched on August 4, 2026.

In groups of 2 to 12 players, you trek across a vast, handcrafted island inspired by the Australian Outback, solving puzzles and uncovering mysteries together. There are no quest markers, stamina bars, or health meters — every discovery is your own.

The world is handcrafted — every inch feels intentional. Radio stations dot the island, each playing its own song you can carry with you on the journey. There is no lore dump: groups often invent their own nicknames for characters and build their own vocabulary for the puzzles.

Big Walk has a defined ending and isn't meant to be played forever — but with a different group of friends, it retains real appeal.

03 — Beginner Guide

How to play

  1. 2 to 12 players, multiplayer only

    There is no single-player mode. Big Walk is entirely designed around teamwork and talking.

  2. The host picks a world version

    Hosts choose a 2-player, 3-player, or 4+ player version of the world — treat this as the minimum number of players. The host keeps the save data, so players can join or drop anytime. Some puzzles adapt to your group size.

  3. Use in-game proximity chat

    The game is built around talking with your friends. Using Discord or Steam voice instead bypasses the puzzle design and makes the game worse.

  4. Handy items

    Walkie-talkies, flashlights, and megaphones — useful, but rarely required. Inventory is limited: you'll need a friend to take your backpack off if you want to consult the paper map inside it.

  5. No quest markers

    No quest markers, stamina bars, or health meters — explore, experiment, and figure things out together.

  6. Ideal group size

    Fewer players makes it a more focused co-op experience; closer to 12 it becomes a fun, chaotic mess. Any size works — the developers didn't design for one ideal number.
